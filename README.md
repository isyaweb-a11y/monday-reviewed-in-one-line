The funniest one-line review of my own Monday, under 200 characters:

> Monday, reviewed: my inbox had 214 unread emails, 3 meetings about meetings, and one calendar invite titled 'quick sync' that lasted two hours.

`main.py` composes the line from an opening, details and a payoff, gates it to 1–200 characters on a single line, and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
